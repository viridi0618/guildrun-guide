## directAnswer

Guildrun does not have a confirmed release date. The Steam store page (L001) currently displays "TBA" as the release date. However, the official developer FAQ posted on the Steam Community hub (L002) states that the developer is aiming for a 2027 release. The game is confirmed for Windows and Mac, with 7 supported languages. An official Discord server exists at discord.gg/guildrun. Steam Deck is playable using mousepad and L1/L2 shoulder buttons, though the developer has not made an official controller promise. Demo progress — including metaprogression, difficulty unlocks, and hero mastery stars — will carry over to the full release. The game will be a premium one-time purchase with price TBD. 2-player co-op is in development. No console ports, PvP mode, or exact launch date have been confirmed.
