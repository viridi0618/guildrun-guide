## directAnswer

The Steam store page confirms there are 300+ relics in Guildrun (L001). Relics are powerful run-modifying choices that can define your strategy. The developer FAQ emphasizes that Guildrun is designed around adaptation (L002) — no single relic strategy works for every run. This guide provides an editorial decision-making framework for evaluating relic choices: understanding trade-offs between immediate power, survivability, economy, and long-term scaling. It does not claim knowledge of specific relic names, exact effects, or guaranteed acquisition methods — those details have not been published by the developer.

### Relics vs Hero Items — Editorial Framework

heading: Relics and Items Serve Different Purposes
body: |
  This is an editorial framework for understanding the strategic difference between relics and hero items, based on common roguelike autobattler conventions:

  | | Relics | Hero Items |
  |---|--------|-----------|
  | Typical scope | Affects entire team or run rules | Affects one specific hero |
  | Strategic impact | Often defines your build direction | Supports your existing direction |
  | Flexibility | May persist for the entire run | May be swappable or replaceable |

  When choosing between a relic or a hero item, consider: does this change my overall strategy (relic), or does it make my current strategy more effective (item)?

### Five Editorial Categories for Relic Evaluation

heading: A Decision-Making Framework
body: |
  For decision-making purposes, this guide groups relic choices into five editorial categories. **These are not confirmed official in-game categories.** They are a framework to help you evaluate relic trade-offs:

  **Immediate Power** — Relics that provide upfront benefits: more damage, faster attacks, stronger abilities. Best when you need to survive the next few fights.

  **Survivability** — Relics that extend your team's staying power: more health, defense, healing, or shields. Best when your team is dying too quickly.

  **Economy** — Relics that improve resource generation: more gold, cheaper shops, bonus items. Best when taken early so they have time to pay off.

  **Long-Term Scaling** — Relics that grow stronger as the run progresses: per-kill bonuses, per-floor stacking, percentage-based improvements. Best when you can survive the early floors without immediate help.

  **Conditional Triggers** — Relics that activate on specific conditions (on kill, on low HP, on ability use). Their value depends entirely on how often your team meets those conditions.

  When offered a relic choice, ask: "What is currently most likely to end my run, and which of these relics best addresses that problem?" Start from your failure condition, not from a tier list.

### Relic Evaluation Principles

heading: How to Think About Relic Choices
body: |
  These principles apply regardless of which specific relics are available:

  **Match the relic to your problem.** If your frontline is dying, a relic that boosts damage does not help. If you cannot kill enemies fast enough, a relic that boosts defense does not help. Diagnose your failure mode first, pick relics second.

  **Rarity does not guarantee value.** A relic that perfectly matches your team's needs is more valuable than a rarer relic that does not — regardless of what color or tier it is labeled.

  **Synergy multiplies power.** Two relics that work together (e.g., one that triggers on kill and another that amplifies kill effects) are more valuable than two standalone relics. Look for relics that enhance each other.

  **Early vs late trade-offs.** Early in a run, immediate power usually matters more — you need to survive. Later in a long run, scaling effects may become more valuable because you have more remaining floors to benefit from them.

  **Pivoting is expensive.** Changing your build direction to accommodate a new relic means previous purchases lose value. Do it when your current direction is failing and the new relic offers a clear alternative — not because the relic looks exciting.

### Evaluating Community Relic Claims

heading: Reading Community Relic Advice Safely
body: |
  Community discussions about "best relics" appear on Steam and Discord. When reading these claims:

  1. **Check the difficulty context.** A relic praised at easier difficulties may be irrelevant at harder ones.
  2. **Check the assumed team composition.** Most claims assume a specific build archetype and relic combination.
  3. **Check the date.** Relics may change with patches.
  4. **Look for corroboration.** One player's experience is not data. Multiple independent reports are more credible.
  5. **Test in your own runs.** The developer FAQ (L002) confirms the game is designed for adaptation — what works for one player's run may not work for yours.

  Treat community relic claims as starting points for experimentation, not as confirmed best practices.

### Common Relic Mistakes

heading: Mistakes Players Make
body: |
  **Chasing rarity.** Picking the highest-rarity relic without checking whether it helps your team.

  **Ignoring conditions.** Taking a relic that only triggers on conditions your team rarely meets.

  **No economy planning.** Taking only combat relics and running out of gold for key purchases.

  **Too many scaling relics too early.** Dying before the scaling pays off because no relics provide immediate help.

  **Pivoting unnecessarily.** Abandoning a working strategy for a relic that looks interesting but does not solve an actual problem.

  **Over-stacking one category.** All defense with no damage leads to timeout losses. All damage with no defense leads to quick deaths.
