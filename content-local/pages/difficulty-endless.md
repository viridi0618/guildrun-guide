## directAnswer

The Steam store page confirms Guildrun features "8 difficulty picks" and an "Endless mode to chase the top of the global leaderboards" (L001). Beyond this official confirmation, specific difficulty names, exact enemy scaling formulas, scoring formulas, and difficulty-specific mechanics have not been published by the developer. Community discussions on Steam describe increasing challenge with each tier, but these reports are anecdotal and not systematically verified. This page documents what is confirmed and provides editorial frameworks for approaching higher difficulties — not a database of difficulty-specific data.

### Higher Difficulty Self-Check

heading: Before Moving to a Higher Difficulty
body: |
  This checklist is an editorial framework — a set of questions to ask yourself before attempting a higher difficulty. It does not describe specific in-game mechanics:

  1. **Are you consistently clearing the current difficulty?** If you are barely scraping through, a harder difficulty will expose every weakness.
  2. **Is your frontline surviving comfortably?** Higher difficulties typically mean more enemy damage. If your frontline barely survives now, it will not survive the next tier.
  3. **Do you have a mix of immediate power and scaling?** Relying entirely on early-game burst or entirely on late-game scaling creates vulnerabilities as difficulty increases.
  4. **Are your relic choices intentional or reactive?** At lower difficulties, random relics can work. At higher difficulties, relic synergy becomes more important.
  5. **Can you identify why a specific run failed?** If you cannot diagnose a loss, you cannot fix it for the next attempt.

### Diagnosing Common Failure Modes

heading: Three Common Failure Patterns
body: |
  When runs fail at higher difficulties, the cause usually falls into one of three categories:

  **Damage too low:** Enemies take too long to kill. Timer pressure becomes constant. Boss phases drag into attrition losses. Solution: assess whether you have enough damage-focused items, relics, and heroes. A team of tanks and healers survives but cannot clear.

  **Frontline collapses:** Your damage-absorbing heroes die early, exposing the backline. Solution: add Defense, HP, or a second frontline hero. A single tank may be sufficient at lower difficulties but inadequate as enemy damage scales.

  **Poor scaling:** Your build was strong early but weakens as enemies grow. Solution: ensure some relics and items provide cumulative benefits (per-kill, per-floor, percentage-based) rather than flat bonuses that lose relevance.

  This diagnostic framework is editorial advice based on general roguelike autobattler principles, not Guildrun-specific data.
