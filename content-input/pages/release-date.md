## directAnswer

Guildrun's free demo launched on July 16, 2026 on Steam (PC) with 25 heroes, 162 items, and over 300 relics. The full game does not have a confirmed release date — no official developer announcement with a specific date or year exists as of this writing. The Steam store page advertises eight difficulty picks, followed by Endless mode for standard ladder runs.

### Demo: What's Available Now

heading: What's in the Demo
body: |
  The Guildrun demo went live on Steam on July 16, 2026. It's free to play, single-player with no timer and no PvP — you can save mid-run and pick up later. Here's what's included:

  - 25 heroes across 7 classes (Warrior, Vanguard, Tank, Duelist, Assassin, Mage, Mystic)
  - 75 specialization paths (3 per hero, for up to 180 specialization options total)
  - 162 items and 300+ relics to discover across runs
  - 8 difficulty picks — seven form the standard ladder (Base through SSS), plus the Red Rift as a separate contract (see the Difficulty Guide for details)
  - Endless Mode with a global leaderboard
  - Rift Challenge Mode with per-class win requirements
  - Weekly patches and an active developer presence since the alpha launched in January 2026

  Source: Steam store page — https://store.steampowered.com/app/3669200/Guildrun/

### Platforms & Languages

heading: Platforms and Language Support
body: |
  **Current platforms**: The demo is exclusively on Steam (PC). The Steam store page lists no console or mobile versions.

  **Language support in the demo**:
  - The Steam store lists English as the supported language.
  - Community posts indicate the demo includes some partial Chinese translation, though users have reported translation quality issues (e.g., a health increase from 75% to 100% was mistranslated as "reduced to 100% health").
  - Japanese is not supported in the current demo, and no developer announcement about future Japanese localization has been made.
