## directAnswer

Guildrun's demo has 25 heroes across 7 classes, and the community is still figuring out who's strong. As of July 22, 2026 — six days after the demo launch — there are no definitive tier rankings backed by systematic data. What exists is early community discussion, with the most active debate centering on **Aria** (reported to "miss everything") and **Sal** (described as "a worse Karsu with a defense buff"). This page captures what players are saying, with sources, and will be updated as the meta settles. If you're looking for a "just tell me who to pick" answer for your first run: **pick a Warrior or Assassin** — they're the most straightforward classes with the least conditional power.

### Community-Favored Heroes (Speculative A-Tier)

heading: Heroes Players Are Positive About
body: |
  Based on Steam community discussion patterns and general player sentiment (NOT systematic analysis):

  **Warriors & Vanguards (Rush carries)**: The community generally agrees that Rush-focused heroes with high base Attack are strong in the early game, where fast kills mean more Shards. Specific hero names are not consistently cited — the class itself is the signal.

  **Tanks with Defense scaling**: Defense has no diminishing returns (EHP = HP × (1 + DEF/100)), making Defense-stacking Tanks extremely durable. Combined with the targeting priority system (Tanks get attacked first), a well-built Tank is the most reliable foundation for any comp.

  **High Attack Speed Duelists**: Attack Speed drives mana generation (+5 mana per hit), and mana drives abilities. High Attack Speed means frequent abilities. The downside is the Duelist animation issue (community report: skills pause auto-attacks, stopping healing from OmniVamp).

  **Karsu** (Warrior): Mentioned positively as a reference point — "Sal is just a worse Karsu with a defense buff." This implies Karsu is the baseline for "good Warrior."

### What Tier Lists Can't Tell You

heading: Why Tier Lists Don't Work Well for Guildrun
body: |
  Guildrun isn't a game where you pick the "S-tier" hero and win. Here's why static tier lists are misleading:

  1. **Hero power depends on items and relics.** A B-tier hero with 3 synergistic items can out-perform an S-tier hero with mismatched items. The item system is half of your power budget.

  2. **Hero power depends on rank.** A rank C Karsu is weaker than a rank S Sal. The shop determines which heroes you can rank up, not which hero is theoretically best.

  3. **Hero power depends on difficulty.** A hero that dominates Index 0 may be useless at Index 6 where modifiers change the rules. At Index 4+, item slots are gated by hero rank — heroes that need many items to function become weaker.

  4. **Hero power depends on the enemy composition.** If you're facing high-Defense enemies, True Damage and Magic (which bypasses Defense for ability damage) gain value. If you're facing squishy enemies, Attack Speed and Crit are better.

  5. **The game is designed for adaptation.** The Steam store description explicitly says: "Adapt each run as different things are offered to you. With infinite build freedom without a PvP meta that would force certain strategies, every run feels like a new puzzle."

  The most useful "tier list" for Guildrun is a decision-making framework, not a static ranking. Know the classes. Know the stats. Evaluate what the shop and Auction House give you. Adapt.

  Source: Steam store page — https://store.steampowered.com/app/3669200/Guildrun/

### Decision Framework (Instead of a Tier List)

heading: How to Evaluate Any Hero in Your Run
body: |
  Instead of a static S/A/B/C ranking, use this decision framework when evaluating heroes in your shop:

  **1. Does this hero match my strategic direction?**
  - Rush comp → look for Warrior, Vanguard, Assassin, Duelist
  - Stall comp → look for Tank, Duelist, Mystic, Mage
  - If the hero doesn't fit, pass — a B-tier hero that fits your comp is better than an S-tier hero that doesn't

  **2. Does this hero fill a gap in my team?**
  - No frontline → prioritize Tank or Vanguard
  - Low damage → prioritize DPS class
  - Dying too fast → prioritize heroes with Defense/HP scaling

  **3. Can I rank this hero up?**
  - If there's a duplicate already in the shop, that's a guaranteed power spike
  - If you already own this hero at a high rank, buying a duplicate is the most efficient Shard spend in the game

  **4. Is this hero's class primary stat something I can itemize for?**
  - If all your items are Attack/Crit and the hero scales with Magic, you're setting up for disappointment
  - If you have items that match the hero's class, that's a synergy worth pursuing

  **5. What difficulty am I on?**
  - At Index 4+, item slots unlock with hero rank — heroes who need many items become harder to use
  - At Index 6, bosses carry buff relics — burst damage and True Damage gain value
